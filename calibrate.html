BT AV Sync setup

1. Microphone access

Chrome only shows speaker names to extensions that have mic access. With it, the extension saves a delay for each speaker and can measure the delay itself. Nothing is recorded or sent anywhere.

2. Tap test

You'll hear 12 beeps, one per second. Tap Space (or click the box) along with the rhythm so your taps land on the beeps, the way you'd clap along to music. Don't wait for each beep and then react. Results are accurate to about ±30 ms.

tap here or press Space

3. Mic test

Plays 3 chirps through your speaker and listens with the laptop mic. Set the volume to normal and keep the room quiet for about 5 seconds.

The mic adds its own small delay. Once you've tuned a delay by ear (or with taps), run the mic test and click the button above. After that, mic results line up with what you hear.
